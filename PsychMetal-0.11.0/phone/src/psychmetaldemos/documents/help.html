PsychMetal 0.11.0

Visual and auditory presentation software for psychophysics, using native Metal and Core Audio on Apple Silicon. The aim is to present the same stimuli on iPhone and Apple Silicon Macs, with Python, Octave or MATLAB on the Mac.

Getting started

Choose a demonstration and tap the large Start demo button at the top. Any settings sit directly below it, followed by the instructions. Export a stimulus uses Create movie. Rotate your device before starting to choose portrait or landscape.

A finger moves the pattern in interactive demonstrations. In Blob array, hold a spot to drag it and lift to leave it. The hidden-ring demonstration uses a second finger to switch between noise and stripes.

Tap with three fingers together to leave a demonstration and return to the menu. Demonstrations run for a limited time.

Rapid flicker

Blob array, Changing noise, Matching flicker and sound, and Frame timing include rapidly changing patterns. If flashing patterns are uncomfortable for you, skip these demonstrations or stop using a three-finger tap.

What to explore

Shapes: every kind of shape and text the toolbox draws, nearly all of it turning, alone or as groups; the turning costs the graphics processor nothing extra.

Blob array: compare the apparent flicker of spots with different frequencies. Labels show the intended frequency for the display’s measured refresh rate.

Drifting Gabors: compare motion within soft-edged Gabor patches. Hidden ring: notice how motion reveals a boundary that can be difficult to see when still. Its start page sets how fast the background noise drifts and how large its cells are.

Movies

Movie playback: play a bundled numbered movie with sound, then draw its frames as a rotating stimulus. Record a stimulus: record generated patterns and clicks, then replay the result. The saved movie follows the intended timing even when saving slows the live display. The movie stays in the app. Use Share recorded movie from the main menu to save or send a copy, without enabling diagnostics. No Photos or camera access is required.

Sound

Matching flicker and sound: a spot flickers from grey to white and a tone swells and fades. Turn the dial to match their timing, or slide the knob to match their rate, then tap Match to see your error; the switches choose what is matched and whether each is seen or heard. Use the speaker or wired headphones: Bluetooth headphones add a delay the phone cannot measure, and the demonstration says so when they are in use.

Sound recorder: a live picture of what the microphone hears. Hold Record while you speak and lift to stop; Play plays it back. The first time, the app asks to use the microphone; you can refuse, and change your answer later in Settings. The sound stays in memory on this device and is discarded when the demonstration ends.

The sound demonstrations run until you tap Quit or tap with three fingers.

Stimulus movies

Movie playback shows a bundled sample. Record a stimulus saves and replays a generated movie. In Export a stimulus, tap Create movie to write a three-second Gabor movie without opening a presentation window. When Movie ready appears, play or share the saved movie. Share recorded movie lets you save or send the latest successful movie. These functions do not use the microphone, camera or Photos. A failed export preserves the previous completed movie.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostic mode starts off. Turn it on to reveal Native timeline, Frame timing, and all report tools. Turning it off hides these tools, including saved reports, without deleting them. All demonstrations collect device and presentation information in Diagnostic mode; many also print timing and drawing statistics. Frame timing provides the detailed graphs. Demos continue through missed refreshes without capturing frames. View last demo output opens the full printed output on a scrollable page. For accurate timing, stop Xcode debugging and launch the app from its home-screen icon.

This is an educational visualization app, not a calibrated clinical instrument. Display behavior varies across devices and settings. Software timestamps do not measure light emitted by the screen.

Support

Visit the project’s issue tracker. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and which demonstration you ran. Avoid including private information.

Built with the open-source PsychMetal toolbox. Version 0.11.0. Demos work offline; opening external support links may use the network.

Reports

Diagnostic runs retain the latest timing report and device/presentation configuration locally. Device & presentation report shows the latest capture, or basic device information before a run. Share reports opens the system share sheet for HTML, plain text and JSON files. You choose the destination; cancelling does not discard your report.
