Privacy policy

PsychMetal Demos · Keith Schneider
Effective October 14, 2026

PsychMetal Demos is an educational app for exploring visual patterns, motion and sound. It does not require an account and has no advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

Touch positions are used to move the visual patterns. Optional diagnostics hold presentation times, touch-event counts and device/presentation configuration. Diagnostics do not capture images. The app does not send this information to the developer or a server.

Microphone

Only the Sound recorder demonstration uses the microphone, and only after you allow it; you can refuse, and change your answer later in Settings. It shows what the microphone hears while the demonstration is open and records only while you hold Record. The sound is kept in memory, is never saved or sent anywhere, and is discarded when the demonstration ends.

The most recent timing report, demo output and device report are saved locally so you can reopen them after restarting. Reports are replaced by newer results. Share reports opens the system share sheet only when you choose it; you decide whether and where to send or save the files. Cancelling keeps the reports on your device. Temporary export files are removed when sharing finishes. The app and its runtime may also keep ordinary local cache or preference files.

Stimulus movies

Movie playback uses a bundled sample. Record a stimulus and Export a stimulus save a movie of generated visual patterns and generated sounds locally in the app; it does not record the microphone or camera. The previous movie is replaced only after the next recording finishes successfully. Share recorded movie opens the system share sheet, where you choose whether and where to save or send a copy. Cancelling leaves the movie in the app. Temporary sharing copies are removed after sharing finishes.

Data collection

The developer does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location through this app. Apart from the microphone for the Sound recorder, the app does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts, or location.

Support and external services

If you choose to email the developer or post on the project’s GitHub page, the information you provide is handled by that service and used to respond to your request. Do not put private information in public GitHub issues. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, or crash-diagnostic information under your Apple settings and Apple’s own policies.
