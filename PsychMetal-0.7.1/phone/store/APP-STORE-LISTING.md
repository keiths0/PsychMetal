## Description

See visual perception in action.

PsychMetal Demos is a collection of interactive demonstrations for students, teachers and anyone curious about vision. Explore contrast, motion and patterns directly on your iPhone or iPad.

• Compare soft spots that pulse at different frequencies, and drag them into new arrangements.
• Watch stripes drift inside Gabor patches: soft-edged patterns used in vision science.
• Explore moving dots and rapidly changing visual noise.
• Reveal a hidden ring through motion and compare noise with stripes.
• Follow short explanations of what to look for in each demonstration.

Demonstrations work offline, with no account, advertisements or subscriptions. Optional diagnostics let you inspect software presentation intervals and captured rendered frames.

Some demonstrations contain rapid flicker. Display behavior depends on your device and settings. This app is an educational visualization tool, not a calibrated clinical or diagnostic instrument.

Created by Keith Schneider using the open-source PsychMetal toolbox and native Metal graphics.
