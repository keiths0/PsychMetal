PsychMetal Demos

Explore how contrast and motion shape what you see.

Getting started

Choose a demonstration, read what to look for, then tap Start demonstration. Rotate your device before starting to choose portrait or landscape.

A finger moves the pattern in interactive demonstrations. In Blob array, hold a spot to drag it and lift to leave it. The hidden-ring and Noise through a mask demonstrations use a second finger to switch between noise and stripes.

Tap with three fingers together to leave a demonstration and return to the menu. Demonstrations run for a limited time.

Rapid flicker

Blob array, Changing noise, and Frame timing include rapidly changing patterns. If flashing patterns are uncomfortable for you, skip these demonstrations or stop using a three-finger tap.

What to explore

Blob array: compare the apparent flicker of spots with different frequencies. Labels show the intended frequency for the display’s nominal refresh rate.

Drifting stripes: compare motion within soft-edged Gabor patches. Hidden ring: notice how motion reveals a boundary that can be difficult to see when still.

Optional diagnostics

Diagnostic mode starts off. Turn it on to run Frame timing and view its graph, or to pause Blob array after a missed refresh and step through captured frames. Capture adds overhead and may cause missed refreshes itself.

This is an educational visualization app, not a calibrated clinical instrument. Display behavior varies across devices and settings. Software timestamps do not measure light emitted by the screen.
